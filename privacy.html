Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

This policy explains what information Vibe Coder Idle (the "game"), made by Morten Falk, uses and why. The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (tokens, upgrades, skills, settings and so on) is saved on your device. It stays there unless you use one of the optional backup or sync features below. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Wipe save in the game, or by uninstalling the game.

Services provided by Google

The game uses these Google services. When it does, Google processes the related data under the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Google Play Billing (optional purchases). If you buy something in the game, Google handles the payment. The game receives only what it needs to deliver your purchase: the product you bought, an order ID and a purchase token. It never sees your payment details. This information is checked on your device and isn't sent to us.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional cloud saves). If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game can store a copy of your save in your Google account so you can continue on another device. Google shares basic Play Games profile information (such as your player ID and display name) with the game for this. We don't receive or store it on any server.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the game's saved progress to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall. You control this in your device settings.

Save codes and the clipboard

The game only copies a "save code" to your clipboard when you tap Copy save code, and only reads it when you tap Import. Save codes stay wherever you choose to paste them.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files outside the game's own storage.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • Local progress: Settings → Wipe save, or uninstall the game.
  • Cloud saves: manage or delete Google Play Games data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.
  • Purchases: your purchase history is kept by Google Play. See Google Play's help pages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
