Works on My Machine

An AI vibe-coding idle game

You don't write code. You write vibes.

An idle game about a developer who never touches the keyboard. Tap PROMPT, squash the bugs your AI leaves behind, hire a team of subagents to do the work for you, and ship it for GitHub Stars.

  • Six AI subagents to hire, fuel and promote
  • Bugs to squash and collect: 16 species, from NullPointer to the Heisenbug
  • Skills to level from 1 to 99, a Dev Rank from Intern to CTO, and 60+ achievements
  • Prestige with Ship It, and permanent Legacy upgrades
  • Works offline, no ads
